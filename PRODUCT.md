# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Osayuki Obazee (Yuki) for software engineering roles. They skim fast, want proof of shipped work, then want the resume or a way to make contact.
Secondary: freelance clients, collaborators, and people who follow his build-in-public content.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Osayuki Obazee ("Yuki"), a full stack developer and founder of MEXO. It exists to get him hired: show real shipped products, his experience, and his public building, then hand over the resume. Success is a recruiter downloading the resume or emailing him.

## Positioning
A developer who ships whole products end to end (frontend, backend, database, deploy) and documents the build publicly. MEXO (a football platform), Yibera (hospital management), AlphaBuild (construction), EcoCycle (Unity recycling game).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4, framer-motion, lucide-react. All copy lives in `src/lib/data.ts` and `src/lib/projects.ts`.
- The content section is fed by a PHP API (`backend/`) with an admin area at `/admin/content`; a `/content` explorer page and `/projects/[slug]` case studies exist.
- Sections that must stay: Hero, About, Experience, Projects, Content, Tech Stack, Contact, Support (Buy Me a Coffee), Footer.

## Brand Commitments
- Colours: green and black (user-binding). All-black ground with green as the brand colour; no light theme.
- Visual references supplied by the user (Nov 2026): Matisse silhouette collage (hero), featured-project + numbered services list (projects), "Let's Talk" black footer, search-first studio stats layout (content), giant ABOUT ME + round portrait and dark intro statement (about), outline "01" numbered service list (experience).
- Name on site: Osayuki Obazee, alias Yuki. Handle @Yuki_dev04.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over claims: shipped products and real screenshots lead.
2. The recruiter path (work, then experience, then resume) is never more than one action away.
3. Building in public is a differentiator, shown with real posts, not described.
