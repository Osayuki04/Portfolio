Scope: homepage `/` (src/app/page.tsx). Mode: Persuade (portfolio for recruiters). Build path: code-led (no image generation).

Audience: recruiters / hiring managers. Action: download resume, then email. Proof: shipped products with real screenshots, experience, public build posts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The developer as a collage of what he shipped. The page is a print-poster gallery on black: oversized grotesk headlines, product screenshots as the material, green as the one ink. Refuses the dev-portfolio default of centred hero + pill badges + equal cards + terminal costume.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #070908 ink-black; one brand green #3CCB7F used as fields (active rows, underline bars, CTA pills), never glow. Archivo variable (wdth 62–125): expanded light for section titles, condensed black for giant words, normal width for body. Geist Mono only for numbers/data. 1px hairlines, full-round pills for list rows and buttons, image tiles with 6px gutters.

STORY: Who he is in one look (portrait inside a mosaic of his products), what he built (featured projects + numbered list), how he works (about + statement), where (experience index), proof of public building (content), stack, contact, close on LET'S TALK.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three columns. Left: stacked display quote "I build / whole / PRODUCTS." Centre: stepped bust-shaped mosaic, portrait as head tile, 8 product screenshot tiles forming shoulders/torso. Right: "Osayuki Obazee" bold with green bar, "SOFTWARE ENGINEER" with bar, then green Resume pill + View work link. Top-left wordmark stack with bar; top-right small nav.

FORM: user-pinned references (7 screenshots), mapped per section; signature interaction: mosaic tiles assemble on load and drift by scroll depth; numbered service/experience rows expand to green pill on hover/focus. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
